# Onchain Traded Funds documentation

The docs extend the app's “Calm Exchange” identity into a Nextra Read surface: a calm technical reference, not a marketing landing page.

## Visual system

- Deep slate and blue-black surfaces define the default dark theme; the light theme preserves the same roles on cool near-white surfaces. Teal is reserved for links, active paths, focus, selection, and confirmed interactions.
- Typography uses an Inter-first system sans stack with no separate display face. Balanced, tightly tracked headings and a 74-character article measure prioritize scanning and sustained reading; code disables ligatures for exact technical strings.
- Thin ledger rules and adjacent surface tones establish hierarchy without decorative shadows or a grid of custom floating cards. The compact OTF mark and restrained 7–8px radii carry the app's form language into the docs.

## Nextra structure and content hierarchy

- The shared Nextra shell supplies the navbar, auto-collapsing sidebar, search, page outline, previous/next navigation, repository and edit links, feedback, and footer.
- The index leads with a factual title and summary, an immediate unaudited warning, a two-column “Start here” directory for protocol, security, risk, and incentives, then a short scope boundary.
- Technical source documents remain factual and are moved into the content collection without rewriting their claims.

## Responsive and accessibility behavior

- At 640px and below, the expanded brand label and navbar “Open app” control are hidden to protect the compact header. The footer stacks vertically and retains its “Open app” link, preserving mobile app access without crowding navigation.
- The document declares English left-to-right content; the decorative OTF mark is hidden from assistive technology. Explicit 2px teal focus rings with 3px offset, themed selection and browser color schemes, and accessible light/dark palettes preserve visibility.
- Dark mode is the default. Reduced-motion preferences effectively disable animations, transitions, and smooth scrolling while preserving every interaction.

## Guardrails

- Keep the overview factual, warning-first, and organized around protocol truth.
- Preserve the readable article measure, explicit focus visibility, mobile footer app link, safety status, and light-theme support.
- Do not turn the index into a promotional hero or use teal as ambient decoration.
