# Onchain Traded Funds

Onchain Traded Funds (OTFs) are experimental ERC-20 basket vaults. A creator fixes the constituent order, raw bootstrap units, fee rates, expense beneficiary, and fund thesis when the vault is created. The contracts do not use prices, calculate NAV, or rebalance.

The application uses a `$1` target and offchain market data to prepare the initial basket. Those inputs are creation metadata, not an onchain price or peg.

<Callout type="warning" emoji="">
  The contracts are unaudited, undeployed on mainnet, and not ready to hold value.
</Callout>

## Scope

These pages describe the current pre-mainnet implementation. They do not certify the contracts or any deployment. The application under `launch/` is a separate prelaunch competition and has no protocol-governance authority.
