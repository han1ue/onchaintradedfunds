# Onchain Traded Funds

Onchain Traded Funds (OTFs) are experimental vaults that hold a basket of ERC-20 tokens. Each vault issues shares in its basket. The creator fixes the basket, initial token quantities, fee rates, fee beneficiary, and fund thesis at creation.

The application uses offchain prices and a `$1` target to prepare the initial basket. The contracts do not calculate net asset value (NAV), maintain a dollar peg, or rebalance.

<Callout type="warning" emoji="">
  The protocol is deployed on Robinhood Chain Testnet. It is pre-mainnet and not ready to hold value.
</Callout>
