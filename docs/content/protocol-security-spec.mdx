# Protocol security specification

The keywords MUST, MUST NOT, and SHOULD describe requirements for the current architecture.

## OTF supply

`OTFToken` MUST mint exactly `1,000,000,000 ether` in its constructor and MUST expose no later mint
path. `MAX_SUPPLY` MUST remain fixed while `totalSupply()` MUST fall when a holder burns owned or
properly allowed tokens. Deployment MUST allocate exactly 100 million to team vesting, 200 million
to the launch manager, and 700 million, or 70% of the original supply, to protocol and community
incentives through the rewards distributor, leaving the deployer with zero OTF.

## Canonical launch

The launch manager MUST create one OTF/WETH V4 pool with static fee 0 and tick spacing 1. It MUST
initialize at the fixed sqrt price representing exactly 20 ETH reference FDV against the original
issuance.

`MAX_SUPPLY` MUST remain canonical in `OTFToken` and MUST NOT be duplicated in the launch
manager. The final tick MUST be `-155311` when OTF is currency0 and `155311` when OTF is currency1,
representing approximately `179.997388091105356396 ETH` reference FDV; the next tick MUST exceed 180 ETH.

The manager MUST receive at least 200 million OTF. Bootstrap and permanent liquidity MUST be the
fixed values `31819848221821239732818` and `21213049526830492717974`.

Tick boundaries and required
token amounts MUST be derived with the pinned V4 `TickMath` and `SqrtPriceMath`, using round-up for
mint requirements and round-down for bootstrap removal. Bootstrap OTF MUST remain at or below 150
million, permanent OTF MUST remain at or below 50 million, and derived bootstrap WETH principal MUST
equal derived permanent WETH.

The hook address MUST encode exactly `beforeInitialize` and `afterSwap`. Every third-party pool
initialization MUST revert, including while the predicted CREATE2 manager address has no code. The
hook MUST NOT add `beforeSwap`.

During bootstrap, `afterSwap` MUST reject final prices outside either
tick-derived boundary. It MUST mark readiness only when current `sqrtPriceX96` exactly equals the
final boundary. While ready, every swap MUST revert. The graduated path MUST return without a pool
state read.

The dedicated launch router MUST use the final boundary for buys and the initialization-side
boundary for sells. It MUST settle only consumed input, preserve unused WETH, refund unused ETH,
enforce minimum output and deadline, and call normal permissionless finalization only after the
PoolManager returns control. A failure in finalization MUST revert the complete boundary purchase.

Finalization MUST recompute derived amounts, remove the bootstrap position, and measure both returned
tokens. It MUST provide only the derived WETH principal and no more than 50 million OTF to fixed
full-range permanent liquidity. Additional WETH MUST remain locked and MUST NOT change sizing.

After
the permanent mint succeeds, the manager MUST burn its complete OTF balance once, including
unsolicited OTF. The permanent NFT MUST remain owned by the manager, and no callable path may remove,
reprice, rebalance, migrate, or replace its principal.

## Team vesting

Live FDV MUST use canonical V4 spot state, the configured ETH/USD answer, and the current token
`totalSupply()`. Both currency orderings MUST produce the same economic price. The oracle answer MUST
be positive, no older than `maxOracleAge`, and not from the future.

Checkpoints MUST round down to a completed $1 million milestone, make 10 million OTF claimable per
milestone through $10 million, and never decrease. Claims MUST go only to the current beneficiary and
MUST NOT exceed the claimable amount less prior claims.

Only the current beneficiary MAY checkpoint, claim, nominate, replace, or cancel a pending successor.
A nonzero nominee MUST explicitly accept before receiving that authority; until then, only the current
beneficiary MAY perform those actions. Acceptance MUST clear the pending nomination without changing
unlocked or claimed accounting. Beneficiary-only checkpointing is an explicit trusted-beneficiary
design choice and MUST NOT be represented as making the V4 spot price manipulation-resistant.

## Fund fees and accounted OTF

Annual creator expense, mint, and redeem rates MUST be immutable and capped at 1,000, 200, and 100
bps. Mint fees MUST apply to direct and routed minting. Redeem fees MUST apply to direct, routed, and
normal in-kind redemption. Shutdown and final emergency exits MUST charge no redeem fee. Preview and
execution functions MUST use identical integer arithmetic.

Every operation that changes accounted OTF MUST checkpoint accrued annual fees first. Direct token
donations MUST NOT change accounted balance or fee benefit. The creator share MUST be:

```text
5000 bps + floor(4000 bps × sqrt(min(accounted OTF, 10m OTF) / 10m OTF))
```

The creator share MUST remain between 5,000 and 9,000 bps. Mint and redeem creator fee shares MUST
round down; annual expense splitting MUST carry its fractional remainder across checkpoints.

All creator and buyback fee shares MUST be minted to the collector. The vault MUST record both
portions with the collector when each fee is charged, and settlement MUST NOT recalculate a split
from later accounted OTF.

No fund-owned constituent OTF may be transferred to the collector merely because it is OTF.

## Native entry and exit

`OTFEntryExitRouter` MUST store a nonzero immutable canonical WETH. Native mint MUST require nonzero
`amountIn` and exact `msg.value == amountIn`; every ERC-20 endpoint MUST reject native value. It MUST
wrap exactly the accepted ETH and use only WETH in basket planning, vault calls, and adapter legs.
Transient WETH refunds MUST be unwrapped and returned as ETH while other constituent refunds remain
ERC-20 transfers.

Native redemption MUST route to canonical WETH, enforce the aggregate WETH minimum, unwrap exactly
the transient output, and send exactly that ETH to the caller. ETH sends MUST be checked and revert
atomically on rejection.

The router MUST accept callback ETH only from canonical WETH, keep every wrap, unwrap, send, and
refund inside the reentrancy guard, clear temporary approvals, and preserve all pre-existing or
forcibly sent ETH and WETH. No native sentinel, zero address, or ETH may reach a vault, `SwapLeg`, or
adapter.

## Buyback collector

The collector MUST accept fee accounting only from vaults recognized by the factory and MUST read
each vault's immutable expense beneficiary when fees are settled.
Unregistered or fake vaults MUST be rejected. Only that beneficiary MAY settle its vault. Settlement
MUST checkpoint annual fees, consume the complete recorded creator and buyback share balances,
and use exactly one beneficiary-selected route: sell the combined registered-vault shares through
approved adapters to canonical WETH, or redeem the combined shares and liquidate every basket
constituent to canonical WETH.

A share-sale route MUST consume the exact pending share balance, MAY use approved intermediate
tokens, and MUST leave only WETH as transient output. Only the configured collector MAY call the
entry router's fee-share sale function. Every leg MUST name exact input and output tokens and a
positive minimum output.

The operation MUST enforce the caller's deadline, minimum aggregate WETH, observed WETH delta,
minimum final OTF, and observed OTF delta. Actual WETH MUST be split proportionally using the stored
fee-share counts. The creator portion MUST be transferred exactly to the beneficiary; only the
buyback portion MAY be spent for OTF. The protocol MUST NOT add an oracle, TWAP, route discovery,
protocol fair-value floor, or hardcoded slippage.

The WETH-to-OTF swap MUST use only the immutable canonical V4 pool key. All OTF acquired by that
execution MUST be burned before success. The collector MUST NOT expose arbitrary calls, token
withdrawals, rescue functions, or configurable final routes.

## Cumulative rewards

The 700-million-OTF incentive allocation MUST be distributed in 208 weekly periods. Week 1 MUST
allocate 14 million OTF, and each later weekly target MUST decline smoothly by approximately 1.968%
according to `14,000,000 × 0.9803203^(n - 1)`, where `n` starts at 1. The schedule MUST NOT use
monthly buckets, quarterly steps, or periodic halvings. No emission MAY be allocated after week 208.

The displayed decay factor is approximate. The publishing process MUST use sufficient precision or
reconcile the final weekly distribution so the 208 periods total exactly 700 million OTF. It MUST
NOT treat the rounded formula's approximately 699,999,574.95-OTF sum as the final aggregate.

Only the configured publisher MAY set a root. A new root MUST be active immediately. Leaves MUST
commit to chain ID, distributor, user, and cumulative entitlement using the documented standard-tree
encoding.

Anyone MAY submit a proof, but tokens MUST go to the committed user. A claim MUST equal the proven
cumulative entitlement less stored prior claims. Invalid proofs, double claims, and decreasing
cumulative values MUST revert. The publisher MUST have no rewards-principal withdrawal path.

The distributor does not enforce the weekly schedule, period caps, eligibility, or APY. Operational
root publication MUST enforce the schedule, while documentation and application displays MUST treat
incentive APY as variable rather than fixed or guaranteed.

## Deployment and verification

All production dependencies MUST be explicit. Production MUST use official Uniswap V4 deployments.
Testnet mirror dependencies MUST match operator-supplied expected bytecode hashes before deployment.
Generated ABIs, frontend configuration, tests, and documentation MUST describe only the current
architecture. Earlier testnet contracts MUST NOT be treated as compatible.
