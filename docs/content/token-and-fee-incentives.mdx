# OTF token economics

OTF has a fixed initial supply and rewards distributed through cumulative claims. Contracts define the token and claim mechanics; the reward publisher controls the emission schedule.

The active September 5 deployment runs on Robinhood Chain Testnet with the 15/135 ETH launch curve. See [Deployment addresses](/deployment-addresses) for the current contracts.

## Supply and allocation

`OTFToken` is an 18-decimal ERC-20 named `Onchain Traded Funds` with symbol `OTF`. Its constructor mints one billion OTF (`MAX_SUPPLY = 1,000,000,000 ether`). It has no later mint, tax, blacklist, seizure, or upgrade function. Holders can burn their own OTF; `burnFrom` requires allowance.

`MAX_SUPPLY` records the original issuance. `totalSupply()` records live supply after burns. The deployment script allocates every token and leaves the deployer with none:

| Destination | OTF | Use |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Team market-cap vesting | 100,000,000 | Ten live-FDV milestones |
| V4 launch manager | 200,000,000 | Bootstrap and permanent liquidity |
| Cumulative rewards distributor | 700,000,000 | 650 million for depositors and 50 million for fund creators, published by multisig |

## Canonical V4 launch

The launch manager creates one OTF/WETH Uniswap V4 pool with a fixed 0% LP fee and tick spacing 1. Its initial price is `0.000000015 WETH` per OTF: a 15 ETH reference fully diluted valuation (FDV) against the original one-billion-token supply.

The pool price is exact at initialization, while each liquidity boundary comes from V4 `TickMath`:

| OTF/WETH ordering | Exact initialization `sqrtPriceX96` | Bootstrap start tick and boundary | Final tick and boundary |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| OTF is currency0 | 9703428570912459262669889 | -180161 / 9703508046175219238931631 | -158188 / 29110022932210076965716350 |
| OTF is currency1 | 646895238060830617511325894307352 | 180161 / 646889939753375374376401562675059 | 158188 / 215633692560272871859121182412411 |

The initial price sits just outside the starting position boundary. The final boundary is the closest tick-derived OTF price below the 135 ETH target, at approximately `134.997562702653186573 ETH` reference FDV.

Permanent liquidity is fixed at `18371007233046122951295`. It pairs just under 50 million OTF with `6.749878135132658333 WETH`. This is the greatest integer liquidity that stays within the 50 million OTF cap after V4 rounding.

Bootstrap liquidity is fixed at `27556748080852150400017` and uses approximately 149,997,417.3963 OTF from its 150 million cap. It is the smallest integer liquidity that returns the permanent position's full WETH principal at graduation. Swap rounding adds 44 or 45 wei of WETH to a purchase that reaches the final boundary, depending on currency order. The [security specification](/protocol-security-spec#canonical-launch) defines the rounding requirements.

The `afterSwap` hook keeps the pool price within the bootstrap boundaries. It marks `GraduationReady` only at the exact final sqrt price; tick equality alone is insufficient. Further swaps revert until finalization.

`OTFLaunchRouter` is the supported bootstrap route. It partially fills buys or sells at the boundaries, takes only the input used, and refunds unused native ETH. A purchase that reaches the final boundary also finalizes graduation after PoolManager returns control. Both steps succeed or the entire transaction reverts. Anyone can also call finalization separately once the pool is ready.

Ordinary Uniswap UI trades can succeed within the corridor. Orders that cross a boundary revert because the standard router cannot supply the required price limit. The launch hook uses `beforeInitialize`, `beforeAddLiquidity`, and `afterSwap`. Normal Uniswap trading resumes after graduation.

Finalization removes the bootstrap position and funds a permanent full-range position with the derived principal. Extra WETH stays locked in the manager and cannot enlarge the position. After the permanent mint succeeds, the manager burns its entire remaining OTF balance, normally about 2,582.6037 OTF plus any unsolicited OTF. The manager owns the permanent NFT and has no withdrawal or replacement function.

Launch reference FDV uses `OTFToken.MAX_SUPPLY()`, so burns do not change the launch targets.

The four phases are `NotInitialized`, `BootstrapActive`, `GraduationReady`, and `Graduated`. Graduation depends on price, with no schedule or countdown.

## Team market-cap vesting

`TeamMarketCapVesting` holds 100 million OTF. Only the current beneficiary can checkpoint it. The contract calculates live USD FDV from:

```text
canonical OTF/WETH V4 spot price
× current ETH/USD oracle price
× OTF.totalSupply()
```

The calculation handles either currency order with full-precision integer arithmetic. It uses the current spot price, which can be manipulated. Beneficiary-only checkpoints do not remove that risk. The ETH/USD answer must be positive, no older than `maxOracleAge`, and not from the future. Testnet uses a fixed $2,000 fake oracle; production requires an explicit oracle address.

Each completed $1 million milestone unlocks 10 million OTF, up to $10 million and the full allocation. Values between milestones round down. Once unlocked, a tranche cannot relock after a price or supply decline.

The beneficiary can claim unlocked tokens minus prior claims and nominate a successor. They can replace or cancel the nomination. Authority transfers only when the nominee accepts, preserving unlocked and claimed amounts.

## Immutable fund fees

Each vault creator selects three rates at creation:

| Fee | Default in the application | Contract maximum |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Annual creator expense | 0% | 10% / 1,000 bps |
| Mint | 0% | 2% / 200 bps |
| Redeem | 0% | 1% / 100 bps |

The rates and expense beneficiary cannot change. The vault collects all three fees by issuing vault shares, recording accrued annual expense at checkpoints. Redemptions after shutdown have no redeem fee. Previews use the same arithmetic as execution.

Holding OTF as an accounted vault constituent changes the division of fee shares, not the fee charged to the investor:

```text
creator share = 50% + 40% × sqrt(min(accounted OTF, 10m OTF) / 10m OTF)
buyback share = 100% - creator share
```

| Accounted OTF | Creator | Buyback and burn |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 50% | 50% |
| 1 million | 62.64% | 37.36% |
| 2.5 million | 70% | 30% |
| 5 million | 78.28% | 21.72% |
| 10 million or more | 90% | 10% |

The contract rounds the creator's percentage down to whole basis points. Mint and redeem creator portions also round down, leaving the remainder for buyback. Annual expense carries fractional split remainders between checkpoints so their frequency does not change the cumulative split.

The vault records accrued expense before accounted OTF changes. Direct donations do not affect the split. OTF held in the fund remains a constituent and is not burned merely because the fund holds it.

`BuybackCollector` receives both portions and records their exact share counts in a separate account for each factory-recognized vault. At settlement, it reads the vault's immutable beneficiary.

## Fee settlement and burn

Only the expense beneficiary can settle a vault's fee account. Settlement records accrued annual fees and consumes all pending creator and buyback shares through one of two routes:

1. Sell the combined vault shares through approved adapters to canonical WETH.
2. Redeem the shares and convert the returned constituents to canonical WETH.

The share-sale route may use intermediate tokens but must consume the exact pending balance and leave only WETH as transient output. The beneficiary supplies routes, minimum WETH and OTF outputs, and a deadline. The collector does not price assets or choose slippage limits.

The collector splits received WETH using the share counts recorded when fees accrued. It pays the beneficiary's portion, rounded down, and spends the remainder on OTF.

Settlement, beneficiary payment, OTF purchase, and burn all succeed or all revert. Selling shares leaves them outstanding with buyers; redeeming burns them and removes their proportional backing.

The final swap uses only the canonical OTF/WETH V4 pool, enforces the deadline and minimum output, and burns every OTF purchased. Buybacks can execute during bootstrap or after graduation. The collector has no arbitrary-call, withdrawal, or rescue function.

## Cumulative rewards and incentive emissions

`MerkleRewardsDistributor` receives 700 million OTF: 650 million for depositors and 50 million for fund creators, or 65% and 5% of the original supply. The publisher's policy distributes this budget weekly over 208 weeks (four years), then ends emissions.

The weekly target starts at 14 million OTF and declines by approximately 1.968% every week:

```text
weeklyEmission(n) = 14,000,000 × 0.9803203^(n - 1)
```

Each week uses the same 65-to-5 split:

```text
depositorEmission(n) = weeklyEmission(n) × 13 / 14
creatorEmission(n) = weeklyEmission(n) - depositorEmission(n)
```

Week 1 assigns 13 million OTF to depositors and 1 million OTF to creators.

`n` starts at 1. The rounded decay factor leaves a shortfall of about 425.05 OTF over 208 weeks. The application applies it for weeks 1–207 and assigns the remainder to week 208, approximately 229,144.55 OTF. The publisher must reconcile the schedule to exactly 650 million depositor OTF and 50 million creator OTF.

Both buckets use capped pro-rata weights:

```text
cappedWeight(i) = min(eligible OTF balance(i), 10,000,000 OTF)
allocation(i) = bucketEmission × cappedWeight(i) / sum(cappedWeight)
```

Depositor weights use eligible deposit balances; creator weights use each creator's eligible OTF balance. Balances above 10 million OTF receive no extra weight. The publisher determines eligibility from weekly snapshots and rounds allocations within each weekly target.

The Funds page shows the combined weekly distribution. Each fund page estimates depositor APY using the depositor bucket, current OTF USD price, and the fund's informational AUM, annualized over 52 weeks. Zero-AUM funds use a labeled $100 calculation baseline without changing displayed AUM. This variable estimate is not guaranteed and does not determine claim entitlements.

`MerkleRewardsDistributor` does not enforce the schedule, allocation split, eligibility rules, or weight cap. Its owner publishes an immediately active cumulative Merkle root with no delay or per-period limit. A valid root can allocate the entire remaining balance ahead of schedule. The owner has no function to withdraw reward principal.

Each standard-tree leaf binds a cumulative entitlement to a chain, distributor, and user. Anyone may submit its proof; payment goes to that user. The distributor pays the proven amount minus prior claims. Invalid proofs, duplicate claims, and entitlements below the amount already claimed revert.

Generate an allocation artifact with:

```bash
corepack pnpm rewards:tree -- \
  --input <allocations.json|csv> \
  --output <artifact.json> \
  --chain-id <id> \
  --distributor <address>
```

The script validates addresses and amount syntax and rejects signed amounts and duplicate users. The publisher must calculate emissions, eligibility, capped weights, and the allocation split before running it, including reconciliation to the 700 million OTF budget.

## Trusted inputs

- The reward-root publisher can allocate the distributor's entire remaining balance through a valid root.
- Each expense beneficiary chooses routes, minimums, and deadlines for its own vault's complete pending fee account.
- The ETH/USD oracle determines one input to live-FDV vesting.
- PoolManager, StateView, PositionManager, Universal Router, Permit2, WETH, adapters, and trading venues execute or report external state used by the protocol.

Production addresses must be supplied explicitly and use official Uniswap V4 deployments. The testnet mirror is accepted only when its runtime code matches the configured hashes.
