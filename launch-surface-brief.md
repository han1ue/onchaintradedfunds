# Launch competition surface brief

- **Mode and job:** Operate. Visitors compare proposed OTFs, inspect their thesis and portfolio shape, then submit or cast progressively unlocked votes.
- **Approved composition:** `.impeccable/mocks/launch-board-a.png`. The first viewport is a dominant ranked leaderboard with concise thesis excerpts and compact allocation strips. A competition timeline sits above it and explains the submission week, voting month, and final review. The ballot workspace extends that desk with one row per proposal and a focused unlock summary rail.
- **Identity:** Extend the incumbent OTF application shell: dark teal default, compact system typography, flat bordered cards, restrained state color, tabular data, and the existing responsive table-to-card behavior.
- **Content rules:** Navigation says “Submit OTF.” The competition begins with 7 submission-only days followed by 30 voting days; submissions remain open during voting. Each eligible account unlocks 3 votes when voting opens, then 1 more every 3 voting days up to 12. The final unlock is voting day 27 and day 30 grants no vote. Every voting transaction requires a fresh public X post, but several unlocked votes may be batched in one transaction and post. Cast votes cannot be moved or removed. Every voting post hides that batch’s OTF picks by default and names them only after an explicit reveal choice. Creators may vote for their own proposal. Do not call votes points or show individual launch dates. Desktop leaderboard allocations pair the segmented portfolio shape with exact percentages; full allocation detail remains on proposal pages.
- **Implementation inventory:** Sticky navigation, competition status and metrics, phase timeline, leaderboard, allocation strip, earned-vote ledger workspace, proposal details, submission wizard, account activity, rules/legal/admin surfaces use semantic HTML/CSS and Lucide icons. OTF and asset marks use existing SVG assets or CSS geometry; no generated raster ships in the product.
