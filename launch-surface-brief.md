# Launch competition surface brief

- **Mode and job:** Operate. Visitors compare proposed OTFs, inspect their thesis and portfolio shape, then submit or distribute a 100-vote ballot.
- **Approved composition:** `.impeccable/mocks/launch-board-a.png`. The first viewport is a dominant ranked leaderboard with concise thesis excerpts and compact allocation strips. The ballot workspace extends that desk with one row per proposal and a focused 100-vote summary rail.
- **Identity:** Extend the incumbent OTF application shell: dark teal default, compact system typography, flat bordered cards, restrained state color, tabular data, and the existing responsive table-to-card behavior.
- **Content rules:** Navigation says “Submit OTF.” Each eligible account has exactly 100 votes, activates its ballot with one X post, may redistribute once every 24 hours without another post, and may allocate votes to its own proposal. The cooldown begins at activation and restarts after each redistribution. Do not call votes points or show individual launch dates. Desktop leaderboard allocations pair the segmented portfolio shape with exact percentages; full allocation detail remains on proposal pages.
- **Implementation inventory:** Sticky navigation, competition status and metrics, leaderboard, allocation strip, 100-vote ballot workspace, proposal details, submission wizard, account activity, rules/legal/admin surfaces use semantic HTML/CSS and Lucide icons. OTF and asset marks use existing SVG assets or CSS geometry; no generated raster ships in the product.
